Individual Figures

Saint Augustine of Hippo

354–430 AD. Grace, original sin, the Two Cities: the theological grammar underlying most of what follows him.

Saint Francis of Assisi

1181/1182–1226 AD. Radical poverty and the imitation of Christ, lived rather than argued.

Saint Thomas Aquinas

1225–1274 AD. The synthesis of faith and reason, natural law, and the Five Ways.
