What Is Theology?

Theology is the systematic, reasoned study of God and religious belief. It doesn't just describe what a tradition teaches, it works out why, traces how doctrine develops over time, and defends it with argument. That's what distinguishes this section from History (narrative events) and Figures (person profiles): each topic page here traces how one specific belief changed across centuries, using a glossary of Church document types, a version-history table, a detailed timeline, and a key-people table.
